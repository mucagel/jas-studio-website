01 — About us

A quiet room for loud progress.

Jas' Studio is a pole and aerial arts studio built around technique, conditioning and artistry. Small classes, attentive coaching and a calm, focused space let every student train at their own pace.

Whether it's your first spin or your next competition routine, we coach the fundamentals first and build from there.
