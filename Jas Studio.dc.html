01 — Our Story

Our Story

Jas’ Studio was founded in 2016 by Jasmin, but the dream behind it began long before the doors first opened.

Before having a studio of her own, Jasmin taught pole fitness in hired venues, including rooms in pubs. Every class meant setting up the poles, teaching, then taking everything down again.

She loved teaching, but always wished she could offer her students something more. A permanent space where nobody had to pack everything away at the end of the night. Somewhere people could feel comfortable, make friends, celebrate their achievements and feel like they belonged.

In 2016, she decided to turn that dream into reality.

And so, Jas’ Studio was born.

But Where Did the Name Come From?

Here’s a little fun fact: Jas’ Studio wasn’t actually the original name!

Jasmin had planned to call it Unique Fitness 4 All and had everything ready to launch under that name.

But her students had other ideas!

Everyone naturally started calling it “Jas’ Studio”. Whether they were heading to class or telling friends where they trained, the name just stuck.

Eventually, Jasmin decided to embrace the name everyone was already using.

So, in a way, Jas’ Studio was named by the very people it was created for.

From Three Poles to Six (and Counting!)

Jas’ Studio began with just three poles, one instructor and Pole Fitness classes.

Today, there are six poles, with plans for nine, and a team of six instructors.

Students can now enjoy Pole Fitness, Aerial Hoop and Pole Choreo, with two distinct Choreo styles: Pole Flow and Heels After Dark.

There’s also Jas’ Studio Kids, offering Pole Fitness and Aerial Hoop classes for younger students.

More Than Just Classes

Jas’ Studio isn’t somewhere students simply turn up for an hour and leave.

Members also have access to Open Training sessions, giving them the opportunity to practise independently, work on their skills and enjoy the studio outside their regular classes.

Not everyone has a pole or aerial equipment at home, so having a dedicated space to train makes a real difference.

The studio also hosts monthly calisthenics workshops, specialist sessions, events and professional photoshoots.

The annual photoshoots have become so popular that what originally took one weekend now takes two!

The Heart of Jas’ Studio

Although a lot has changed since 2016, the reason Jasmin opened the studio hasn’t.

To create a welcoming, supportive space where people can challenge themselves, discover what they’re capable of and feel part of something special.

For many students, Jas’ Studio has become more than somewhere to exercise. It’s a place to escape the stresses of everyday life, make lasting friendships and feel completely at home.

A second home for some, and a home for those who need one.

Because at Jas’ Studio, it’s not just about what you achieve on the pole or in the air.

It’s about the people, the confidence and the memories made along the way.

01 — Our Story

Our Story

Jas’ Studio has been welcoming students since 2016, but in February 2026, an exciting new chapter began with the launch of Jas’ Studio Kids!

After years of providing Pole Fitness and Aerial Arts for adults, it was time to open the doors to a younger generation.

Jas’ Studio Kids was created to give children their own space to discover the exciting world of pole fitness and aerial hoop in a fun, welcoming and supportive environment.

Our classes are designed to help children develop strength, coordination, flexibility and confidence, all while learning new skills, making friends and, most importantly, having fun!

Little Beginnings, Big Possibilities

Although Jas’ Studio Kids is still in its early days, it shares the same values that have been at the heart of the studio since the beginning.

We believe every child deserves somewhere they can feel included, encouraged and proud of what they achieve.

From their very first spin to mastering a new move, every little milestone is something worth celebrating.
